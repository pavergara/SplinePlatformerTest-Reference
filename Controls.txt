Keyboard
========

-Menu
Up/Down/Left/Righ: WSAD
Accept: Space/Enter
Back: Q

- Gameplay
Left/Right : A/D
Crouch: Left Shift
Jump: Space
Pause: Tab


Gamepad:
========

-Menu
Up/Down/Left/Righ: Left Stick
Accept: FaceButton Bottom (A)
Back: FaceButton Right (B)

-Gameplay
Left/Right : Left Stick Left/Righ
Crouch: RightShoulder Button
Jump: FaceButton Bottom (A)
Pause: Special Right (Start)
